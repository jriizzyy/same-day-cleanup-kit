# Same-Day Cleanup Kit

Fast remote cleanup for messy documents, resumes, flyers, menus, spreadsheets, PDFs, and process notes. Small jobs can usually be scoped the same day, with a preview before final delivery.

## What I can clean up

- Resumes and cover letters
- Event flyers, menus, and one-page PDFs
- Messy copied text or rough notes
- Spreadsheets, CSVs, and simple Google Sheet structures
- SOPs, checklists, intake forms, and small process docs

## Packages

- **Basic Cleanup, $25:** simple formatting, structure, and readability cleanup for one small document.
- **Standard Cleanup, $35:** polished document, PDF-ready formatting, and one revision.
- **Rush Bundle, $50:** same-day priority cleanup plus one matching add-on, such as a caption, checklist, second version, or simple template.
- **SOP Kit, $55:** SOP plus checklist, intake form, and client-facing template.

## Turnaround

Most small projects are delivered within 24 hours after source material and scope are confirmed. If you need help today, open a request with the deadline and desired output format.

## How it works

1. Send the rough file, text, screenshot, or description.
2. I confirm scope and price.
3. I create a preview.
4. You pay through a simple invoice or payment link after approving the preview.
5. I send the final editable file or PDF.

## Boundaries

- No fake resume experience, fake credentials, or misleading claims.
- No account logins or account setup tasks.
- No sensitive datasets unless the scope is clearly safe and appropriate.
- No spam, fake reviews, or pass-through tasks.
